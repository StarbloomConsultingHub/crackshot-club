The Arcade

Rainy day? Winter is Workshop Season? Step up to the counter. Two original browser games built for the club. They're free, there's nothing to install, and they play with a mouse or a fingertip.

Gallery Row

A carnival shooting gallery. Three conveyor belts of cast-iron style ducks, rabbits, and owls, plus brass bells, spinners, gold ducks, and a sliding cracker bullseye. You get one tube of 20 shots per round for 3 rounds, and the belt speeds up each round. String hits together for a streak multiplier.

Play Gallery Row

Crack Shot

Our thrown-cracker game. Round, scalloped crackers fly from the trap in pairs, and you break them with a smoothbore shot pattern. You get 3 shots per pair, and a sleeve is 25 crackers, just like the Cracker Count. Break enough to earn the next, faster sleeve, and try for a Clean Sleeve. Crumbs the crow is watching.

Play Crack Shot

How to play

  • Mouse: aim with the pointer and click to shoot.
  • Phone or tablet: tap right on the target. Both games work held upright or sideways.
  • Keys: P or Esc pauses, M mutes, and Space starts. There are also pause and sound buttons in the top bar.
  • High scores are saved only in your browser. Copy my score puts a line of text on your clipboard so you can paste it on the message board. Nothing gets posted for you.
