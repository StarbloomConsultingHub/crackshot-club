The gun: Henry H1 Garden Gun

Henry Repeating Arms makes the only current production smoothbore .22 rimfire we know of: the H1 Garden Gun, model H001GG-22 in .22 LR and H001GG-22M in .22 WMR. It has an 18.5-inch smoothbore barrel and is 36.5 inches overall. Henry sells only through licensed dealers.

Vintage smoothbores

Targo, Routledge-bored Remingtons, Stevens 26½, Winchester and Marlin smoothbores turn up at auctions and estate sales. Buy only through a licensed dealer or a legal transfer in your state. Collector talk goes in the Smoothbore Registry thread on the message board.

Ammunition

.22 LR or .22 WMR shot shells (often sold as "pest control" or "snake shot") from the major makers. Check your gun's manual and the maker's guidance.
