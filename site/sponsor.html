A small club. A shared collection.

Keep the history within reach.

22 Crack Shot brings together smoothbore rimfire history, the old shooting galleries, and original browser games. Sponsorship helps cover the everyday cost of keeping that collection online.

We are starting small: a limited number of clearly marked placements, agreed for a fixed term. The aim is to cover costs and keep the Halls and arcade free to explore.

01 / A considered placement

Room to be seen.

A compact panel on the homepage, with additional placements at the end of the museum pages and in the arcade lobby. On phones, the panel follows the main content.

02 / A simple arrangement

A term we agree on.

We agree on the message, placement, dates and a fixed contribution before a campaign begins. Paid promotional panels carry an Advertisement label.

03 / An independent collection

History stays independent.

Support helps keep the site running. Exhibit research, corrections and source credits remain the club’s editorial responsibility.

Support something worth keeping.

If your organization shares an interest in preserving this history or supporting a small enthusiast community, we would be glad to discuss a suitable placement.

Simple, modest reporting.

When impression reporting is enabled, we count a panel after at least half of it has been on screen for one continuous second in a visible tab. Counts are estimates of displayed panels, not unique people or guaranteed reach.

Our counter uses no advertising cookies or member identities. It stores daily totals, short-lived random impression IDs, and a short-lived keyed network digest to limit repeat requests. It does not store raw IP addresses or share visitor records with sponsors.
