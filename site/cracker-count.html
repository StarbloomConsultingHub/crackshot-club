The Cracker Count

The club's house challenge. Shoot a string, count your breaks, post your card.

The string

  • A string is 25 crackers ("a sleeve").
  • Round, scalloped, store-bought crackers. Any brand.
  • A break is any visible piece off the cracker. Chips count; misses don't.

Events

Gallery Row

Crackers set on a printed cracker rail, shot in order like an old gallery row.

Mini Trap

Crackers thrown from a club-style mini trap, Targo style.

Station distance and league details will be set in the league rules before the Spring League.

Proof and scoring

One uncut video plus a photo of your score card. Honor system, with spot checks. Score cards and the leaderboard are coming soon.

Badges

  • Clean Sleeve: 25 for 25.
  • Crumb Bum: most chips in a month.
  • Midway Regular: posted a string three months running.

Seasons

Spring League (Mar–May 2027) · Summer Midway (Jun–Aug) · Fall Harvest (Sep–Nov) · Winter is Workshop Season: designs, history, and collecting.
