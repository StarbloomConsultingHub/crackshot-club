Pull the Sleeve

The club's house challenge. Count the sleeve, shoot every cracker, post your card.

The round

  • Count the sleeve first and write that number on your card. A current retail sleeve of round, scalloped, store-bought crackers is usually about 30. Foodservice sleeves are often 32. Use what you actually have. Any brand.
  • Shoot every cracker in that sleeve. Don't add from the next pack, and don't stop at 25.
  • Score is breaks divided by crackers in the sleeve, as a fraction and a percent (for example, 28/31 = 90.3%). Rank by percent, then by number of breaks if the percents tie.

The Short Stack

Same rules, one small pack: typically a Fresh Stacks pack of 13, or any short pack of about 12–13. Count the pack, shoot the whole pack, score as a percent. It's the low-cost, short-format round — not a youth event. The club is 18+ only. Short Stack and Pull the Sleeve scores don't share a board.

Gallery Row

Mini Trap

Crackers thrown from a club-style mini trap, Targo style. Singles are the default; a true double is optional, with each cracker scored.

Allowed if you have no trap. Note it on the card.

What counts as a break

A break is any visible piece knocked off the cracker, like a clay visible-piece rule. Dust, a wobble, or a hole with no piece off is a miss. A cracker already broken in the pack, or broken by the trap, is a no-bird: replace it from the same box and don't count the broken one. Flying fragments are not new targets.

Proof and scoring

Honor system. A witness's initials help when you have a witness. Photograph the filled score card and post it to the club noticeboard. Video is optional. A leaderboard is coming.

Badges

  • Clean Sleeve: 100% of the sleeve you actually shot.
  • Clean Stack: 100% of a Short Stack pack.
  • Crumb Bum: most chips in a month.
  • Midway Regular: posted a string three months running.

Printable score cards

Print a card, fill it on the line, and photograph it when you're done. Navy on cream; the cards also print cleanly in black and white. Mark X for a break, O for a miss, and leave unused boxes blank.
