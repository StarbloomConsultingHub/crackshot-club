# Pull the Sleeve score cards (November 2026)

Letter-size printable score cards for the club's house challenge, **Pull the Sleeve**, and the short-format round, **The Short Stack**.

- `pull-the-sleeve-scorecard.pdf` — one Letter page: Pull the Sleeve (boxes 1–34) plus a Short Stack mini-section with a cut line. SVG and PNG previews sit beside the PDF.
- `short-stack-halfsheet.pdf` — two Short Stack half-sheets on one Letter page, with a center cut line.

Navy on cream; prints cleanly in black and white. Mark **X** for a break, **O** for a miss, and leave unused boxes blank. Count the sleeve or pack first.
