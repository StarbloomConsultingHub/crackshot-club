# 22 Crack Shot — sponsorship brief

## The placement

A clearly labeled, static promotional panel on the desktop homepage, following the content on phones. Additional placements are available after the two Halls and on the arcade lobby. One campaign appears per placement. The club approves text and artwork, and preserves its independent editorial decisions.
