# Smoothbore Hall completeness audit: factory .22 smoothbores

Prepared Fri Nov 20, 2026 (ET) for Darren's question: *"Are you sure we have covered every production model smoothbore .22 rifle ever made?"*
Research only; no site content was changed by this audit. The inventory was read from `origin/main` @ `0efc149` (`site/assets/halls/smoothbore.json`, `site/hall.html`, `site/gallery-hall.html`, `site/gallery-atlas.html`).

**Short answer:** No. The hall covers the core factory guns well: the Stevens boys' rifles, Mo-Skeet-O Remington and Winchester, Targo, the 572SB, the Nylon 10 SB, the 25MG and the Henry. But it misses at least **6 confirmed factory .22 smoothbores**, has **4 likely ones** to chase, and has one factual claim that's wrong: the hall says the Henry is "the only smoothbore .22 in current production that we know of," but Savage's Mark I-G Smooth Bore was still being sold new in 2025–26.

## Confidence labels
- **Confirmed**: printed in a factory catalog or price guide (Standard Catalog, Gun Digest/Blue Book), on a manufacturer page, or in an auction lot with a factory barrel marking.
- **Likely**: several independent owner or forum reports with matching factory markings, but no catalog page found yet.
- **Rumor**: a single mention, or the evidence points to a conversion or a shot-out bore.

## Collector tier key (suggested; Darren collects on a budget)
| Tier | Rough street money | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Holy Grail** | $1,500+ or a handful known | Museum or auction-only. Display piece. |
| **Prize** | $500–$1,500 | Worth saving up for. Rare enough to brag about. |
| **Solid Find** | $250–$500 | Turns up at gun shows if you know the stamp. |
| **Bargain Beater** | under $250 | A shooter. The club's workhorse. |

Prices are snapshots from the sources cited. They are not appraisals, and older price-guide numbers (2008 Standard Catalog, 2011 Blue Book) are stale.

## (b) Confirmed factory .22 smoothbores MISSING from the hall

### 1. Remington Model 341SB "Sportsmaster" smoothbore. **Confirmed.**
- **Era / maker / action:** 1936–1940 (parent 341), Remington, bolt-action tube-magazine repeater.
- **How to ID:** Remington Society: "intended for .22 caliber Long Rifle shot cartridges only," with a white metal shotgun-style bead and no rear sight; barrel stamped for .22 shot cartridges. The Standard Catalog calls it "the so-called 'Routledge' smooth bore," so check the bore for a Routledge step.
- **Value:** Standard Catalog (2008) Exc $275 / Good $225 / Poor $85. A Remington Society forum estimate for a shooter-grade gun is $125–150 (undated). No recent sold comp captured.
- **Tier:** **Solid Find** (Bargain Beater if worn).
- **Sources:** Standard Catalog of Remington Firearms (Krause, 2008), "Model 341SB" entry: https://ia800808.us.archive.org/29/items/pricing/Standard%20Catalog%20of%20Remington%20Firearms.pdf · Remington Society, "Remington 'Sportsmaster' and Me": https://remingtonsociety.org/remington-sportsmaster-and-me-best-friends · RSA forum t=724: https://www.remingtonsociety.org/forums/viewtopic.php?t=724 · Chuck Hawks: https://chuckhawks.com/remington_341_rifle.htm

### 2. Remington Model 580SB. **Confirmed.**
- **Era / maker / action:** Parent 580 made 1968–1978 per the Standard Catalog. One auction lot was a 1974-vintage 580SB. Remington, bolt single shot.
- **How to ID:** 24" smooth barrel, bead front sight, **no provision for a rear sight**, "22 L. RIFLE SHOT ONLY." Monte Carlo stock like the standard 580.
- **Production:** One YouTube video claims about 3,000 smoothbores out of about 60,000 Model 580s. **Unverified.**
- **Value:** Standard Catalog (2008) NIB $200 / Exc $125. Truegunvalue shows a 1969 listing at $495.07 (sold date unclear). Guns International has a NIB example.
- **Tier:** **Bargain Beater to Solid Find.** This is probably the most findable vintage smoothbore for the club.
- **Sources:** Standard Catalog (above), "Model 580SB" · Guns International NIB listing: https://www.gunsinternational.com/guns-for-sale-online/rifles/remington-rifles---single-shot/remington-model-580-sb-smooth-bore-new-in-the-box.cfm?gun_id=103442213 · Amoskeag lot (1974 gun): https://live.amoskeagauction.com/lot-details/index/catalog/51/lot/27364/Remington-Model-580-SB-Bolt-Action-Smoothbore-Rifle · Metzger lot: https://www.bidmetzger.com/auctions/31532/lot/1825078-remington-model-580-smooth-bore-22-lr-shot-rifle

### 3. Remington Model 572 BDL Smoothbore. **Confirmed.** It could be added to the 572SB exhibit rather than getting its own.
- **Era / maker / action:** Standard Catalog: "Introduced as a special production item in 2007." Still on Remington's site in a Feb 2010 Wayback capture. Remington, pump, tube magazine.
- **How to ID:** BDL deluxe furniture with a **21" smoothbore, bead-sighted barrel** "capable of handling 22 shot cartridges."
- **Value:** Standard Catalog NIB $427 (2008). Bud's Gun Shop has had a listing. No sold comp captured.
- **Tier:** **Prize** (a late, low-volume deluxe pump).
- **Why it matters:** It disproves the hall's line that the 572SB was "the last factory smoothbore pump from a big U.S. maker, until the Henry."
- **Sources:** Standard Catalog (above), "Model 572 BDL Smoothbore" · Remington product page (Wayback, Feb 2010): https://web.archive.org/web/20100612062414/http://www.remington.com/products/firearms/rimfire/model-572/model-572-bdl-fieldmaster.aspx?panel=1 · Bud's listing: https://www.budsgunshop.com/products/rifles/remington-model-572-bdl-smoothbore-22-short-22-long-22-lr-blue-black-rifle-15-round-21-inch-99829

### 4. Remington No. 6 Rolling Block, smoothbore. **Confirmed** (the hall currently has it only as a "likely" .32 in the finale).
- **Era / maker / action:** 1902–1933 for the No. 6 family (about 498,000 of all variants). Remington, rolling-block single shot, takedown.
- **How to ID:** Standard Catalog: "It was also produced with a smoothbore barrel to be used with shot cartridges." 20" round barrel. The catalog includes a photo captioned "Remington 'No. 6' Rifle, Smooth Bore" (Goodwin, *The Guns of Remington*). Calibers per the catalog: .22 rimfire and .32 Short/Long. Which calibers the smoothbore came in is not stated (RSA forum question unresolved).
- **Value:** No smoothbore-specific price found. A standard No. 6 is V.G. $800 / Good $350 / Fair $100 (Standard Catalog 2008). Truegunvalue shows a "Scarce Remington Model 6 .22 S L LR Smooth Bore" listing title (no reliable price).
- **Tier:** **Solid Find** (beware shot-out rifled bores, which are common on these).
- **Sources:** Standard Catalog (above), "No. 6 Rolling Block Rifle" · RSA forum "Model 6 shotgun?": https://remingtonsociety.org/forums/viewtopic.php?t=3230 · Truegunvalue: https://truegunvalue.com/rifle/model-6l/price-historical-value/used/2

### 5. Savage Mark I-GSB / Mark I-G Smooth Bore. **Confirmed.** It is modern and possibly still in production.
- **Era / maker / action:** Modern Savage (Mark I-G introduced 1996 per Gun Digest; smoothbore start year not found). Savage, bolt single shot, AccuTrigger on current examples.
- **How to ID:** Model marked Mark I-GSB / "Mark I G Smooth Bore," 20.75–21" smooth barrel, chambered "22 LR Shot Cart." on the California DOJ roster. UPC 062654507038, Savage #50703.
- **Status conflict:** Gun Digest's value entry says "Discontinued," but retailers listed it new in 2025–26 (PSA/KYGUNCO deal $236.99, Oct 2025; Bud's; WikiArms from $249.75). Treat it as **current or recently current**.
- **Value:** About $237–$305 new (2025–26 retail listings).
- **Tier:** **Bargain Beater.** This is the cheapest new factory smoothbore .22 and a good club loaner.
- **Sources:** Gun Digest Gun Values, Mark I & II series: https://gunvalues.gundigest.com/savage-arms-corporation/mark-i-ii-series · CA DOJ roster: https://oag.ca.gov/firearms/certified-firearm/savage-mark-i-gsb · Ammoland deal post (Oct 2025): https://www.ammoland.com/2025/10/savage-arms-mark-i-g-smooth-bore-22lr-single-shot-bolt-rifle · Bud's: https://www.budsgunshop.com/products/rifles/savage-arms-mark-i-g-smooth-bore-22-lr-blued-rifle-20-75-inch-24322

### 6. Winchester Model 62 / 62A "Smooth Bore" (.22 Long Rifle Shot). **Confirmed to exist; extremely rare and possibly experimental or limited.**
- **Era / maker / action:** About 1940. Winchester, visible-hammer pump.
- **How to ID:** Documented example s/n 103592 has a 23" smooth, **non-counterbored** barrel marked "Model 62 .22 LONG R. SMOOTH BORE A." Schwing's *Winchester Slide-Action Rifles* pictures an experimental 62 "Long Rifle Shot," s/n 101551 (early 1940), with no sight grooves. A forum contributor counts about 8 known, most near that serial range. LSB Auctions sold a 1940 "factory smooth bore" 62.
- **Value:** No price captured (the LSB page would not load).
- **Tier:** **Holy Grail.**
- **ID caution:** A shot-out rifled 62 is not this. Look for the factory "SMOOTH BORE" barrel stamp, and ask Cody Firearms Museum records for a factory letter.
- **Sources:** Winchester Collector forum: https://winchestercollector.org/forum/winchester-22-rim-fire/winchester-model-62-smooth-bore · Rimfire Central cross-post: https://www.rimfirecentral.com/threads/winchester-model-62-smooth-bore.987369 · LSB Auctions lot: https://lsbauctions.com/rare-winchester-model-62-smooth-bore-pump-action-takedown-rifle

## (d) Entries to relabel, correct, or drop

1. **Henry H1 exhibit and hall timeline: "the only smoothbore .22 in current production that we know of." Correct this.** Savage's Mark I-G Smooth Bore was sold new in 2025–26. Suggested wording: "one of the few smoothbore .22s still sold new (along with Savage's Mark I-G Smooth Bore)."
2. **Remington 572SB: "the last factory smoothbore pump from a big U.S. maker, until the Henry lever gun." Correct this.** Remington's 572 BDL Smoothbore came out in 2007. Either fold the BDL Smoothbore into this exhibit (dates "1961–79; BDL Smoothbore from 2007") or give it its own exhibit.
3. **Remington 510/511/512 SB: relabel confidence.** 510SB is confirmed (Standard Catalog; Blue Book lists "Model 510 Routledge/Smoothbore," $300–450). 511SB and 512SB should read "reported by owners; not in the Standard Catalog."
4. **Remington Nylon 10 SB: replace "forum talk: ~200 guns, unverified"** with the Standard Catalog figures: about 10,700 Model 10s made 1962–64, about 2,000 smoothbore, only about 200 of those with the 24" barrel; SB barrels are marked "smoothbore." The 2008 value was SB Exc $1,000, plus 100% for 24".
5. **Finale (.32 honorable mention): upgrade the Remington No. 6 from "likely" to "confirmed smoothbore."** The Standard Catalog says a smoothbore No. 6 was made, but it doesn't say which calibers the smoothbore came in, so keep ".32 smoothbore" as unconfirmed. Keep No. 4 as unverified.
6. **Winchester 36 and Chiappa Little Badger: keep them, but tag both clearly as "9mm Flobert cousin, not .22"** in the exhibit tags or filter. Both already say "cousin" in the summary. **The "(and earlier RC 92)" in the Chiappa title has no source in the exhibit.** Source it or drop it. This pass found nothing on an RC 92 9mm shotgun.
7. **Winchester 61 "Shot Only": relabel the classification** as "shot-only (Routledge stepped bore or counterbore)" rather than a plain smoothbore. The exhibit text already explains this; it's just a tag or filter issue.
8. **"Famous, but not smoothbores" card for the Winchester 1890/1906/62:** add one line saying a tiny number of factory "SMOOTH BORE"-marked Model 62s (about 1940) exist, so the card isn't read as "no 62 was ever smooth."
9. **Henry capacity:** the exhibit says 14 (LR) / 11 (WMR) per Henry's spec table. Henry's own 2024 video says 15 rounds (LR), and GrabAGun lists the WMR at 12+1. **Re-check Henry's current spec page** before changing it.
10. **Hall timeline "1930s: Mo-Skeet-O" paragraph:** add the 341SB and 41SB to the Remington list. Consider a "1960s–70s" line for the 580SB.
11. **Nothing to drop outright.** No current .22 exhibit turned out to be a non-factory gun.
