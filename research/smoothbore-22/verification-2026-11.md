# Verification of PR 7

This review supersedes the original audit's confidence labels and suggested site changes. It checks the smoothbore catalog audit, not the separate challenge-count note. The original audit is retained as a dated research record.

**Finding:** the hall was incomplete, but “six confirmed missing factory .22s” was too precise. That tally mixed a later variant of an existing exhibit with a rolling-block entry whose smoothbore chamberings were not established. Some proposed downgrades were incorrect. Factory evidence confirms the Remington 33, 511SB, 512SB, No. 4 and No. 6, and Chiappa RC92. Additional overseas leads mean neither audit establishes a complete worldwide inventory.

## Decisions and evidence

| Subject | Verification | Hall decision |
|---|---|---|
| Remington 341SB | **Confirmed.** The [manufacturer's historical model record](https://web.archive.org/web/20250720023854/https://www.remarms.com/firearm-history/model-341-speedmaster) names the SB for .22 LR shot. The [RSA journal, first quarter 2011, printed p. 37](https://remingtonsociety.org/remington-sportsmaster-and-me-best-friends/) describes the sights. Its family dates are 1936–1940; a complete SB production span is not established. | Add a card with variant photograph. |
| Remington 580SB | **Confirmed.** [Remington's record](https://web.archive.org/web/20250720023839/https://www.remarms.com/firearm-history/model-580) lists the SB and dates the **parent family** to 1967–1978, rather than the audit's 1968 start. Its approximately 60,000 total covers the family. The claimed 3,000 SB total remains unverified. | Add a card; separate family years and production count from SB history. |
| Remington 572 BDL Smoothbore | **Confirmed.** The [Spring 2007 factory catalog, printed pp. 18–19, PDF pp. 20–21](https://bayanay.ru/forum/data/cats/Spring2007.pdf#page=20) calls it new and describes a **22-inch** barrel, not 21 inches. The 21-inch figure belongs to later specifications, not that introduction catalog. | Expand the 572 card and remove the “last pump until Henry” claim. Do not imply continuous production since 2007. |
| Remington No. 4 and No. 6 | **Confirmed smoothbore options; separate .32 specimens also documented.** The [factory catalog excerpt, printed p. 88, reproduced in Remington's Model 4 historical document](https://www.remarms.com/sites/default/files/2026%20Revised%20Historical%20OMs/Model%204%20Rolling%20Block%20%28REV%200426%29.pdf) offers both as smoothbores. Its list of family calibers does not prove that every caliber was sold in the smoothbore configuration. The 2026 cover revision is not the original catalog date. Separate [Amoskeag 2019 lot 433](https://live.amoskeagauction.com/lot-details/index/catalog/25/lot/13693/Remington-No-4-Smoothbore-Rolling-Block-Rifle) and [John Dunn’s firsthand No. 6 account, September 2020, pp. 22–24](https://www.ssaa.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/September_2020_web.pdf#page=24) identify .32 smoothbores with barrel markings. | Correct the finale using both factory and specimen evidence. Do not count a proven .22 variant solely from the family caliber list. |
| Savage Mark I-GSB | **Confirmed, currently catalog-listed.** [Savage SKU 50703](https://savagearms.com/firearms/sku/50703) explicitly says Smooth Bore in its specification table, with a 21-inch barrel. [California DOJ](https://oag.ca.gov/firearms/certified-firearm/savage-mark-i-gsb) independently identifies .22 LR shot cartridges. Generic rifled-family copy on Savage's page conflicts with the SKU table, so use the SKU-specific field and independent identification. | Add a card. Say catalog-listed as checked November 20, 2026; retail stock is not proof of continuous production. |
| Winchester 62 Smooth Bore | **Confirmed documented variant, limited evidence.** The [2017 WACA specimen account and photographs](https://winchestercollector.org/forum/winchester-22-rim-fire/winchester-model-62-smooth-bore/) show the marked barrel and describe a 23-inch non-counterbored example. The [archived LSB specimen record](https://lsbauctions.com/rare-winchester-model-62-smooth-bore-pump-action-takedown-rifle/) identifies another factory smoothbore. The forum's approximate-eight remark is not a production census or eight independently verified identical guns. | Add a qualified specimen-based card; distinguish it from rifled Gallery configurations in both halls. |
| Remington 510 / 511 / 512 | **Confirmed factory designations.** Remington lists [510SB](https://www.remarms.com/firearm-history/model-510-targetmaster), [511SB and a separate 511 Routledge Bore](https://web.archive.org/web/20250720023844/https://www.remarms.com/firearm-history/model-511-scoremaster), and [512SB](https://web.archive.org/web/20250720023852/https://www.remarms.com/firearm-history/model-512-sportsmaster). The 511/512 pages contain a copied “510SB” typo in their caliber field, but explicitly name the correct variants under Variations. Absence from one price guide is not grounds to downgrade them. | Retain all three as confirmed; replace secondary citations with manufacturer records. |
| Nylon 10 SB | **Variant confirmed; quantities remain estimates.** Dan Shideler's *Standard Catalog of Remington Firearms* (Krause, 2008), [printed p. 115](https://archive.org/download/pricing/Standard%20Catalog%20of%20Remington%20Firearms.pdf#page=116), estimates 10,700 Nylon 10s, around 2,000 SBs, including around 200 long-barrel guns. [Blue Book](https://bluebookofgunvalues.com/products/remington-nylon-10-sb-single-shot) instead gives 2,064 short-barrel SBs and an unknown long-barrel count. Neither is a factory ledger inspected in this review. | Attribute the numbers and disclose the disagreement; do not promote the 200 figure to settled fact. |
| Henry H1 | **Current specifications confirmed.** [Henry's specification sheet, PDF pp. 6–7](https://www.henryusa.com/media-kit/firearm-specifications/lever-action-shotguns/) gives 14 rounds for H001GG-22 and 11 for H001GG-22M. Its [2019 launch](https://www.henryusa.com/news/the-garden-gun-makes-a-comeback-with-new-henry-offering/) described the original H001GG. The [2026 substitution list](https://www.henryusa.com/media-kit/2026-h1-suggested-substitution-list/) differentiates the newer designation. | Keep 14/11; clarify that 2019 dates the family, and remove exclusivity. |
| Chiappa RC92 | **Confirmed, not an unsupported title.** Chiappa's [2012 Magalog, printed p. 98, PDF p. 51](https://www.all4shooters.com/it/tiro/cultura/chiappa-magalog-2012/magalog-chiappa-firearms.pdf?cid=mox#page=51) and [September 2013 manual, p. 6](https://www.taylorsfirearms.com/media/binaryanvil/media_library/CHIAPPA_LITTLE_BADGER_RC92_DOUBLE_BADGER_RIFLE_MANUAL.pdf#page=6) separate 6 mm and 9 mm Flobert smoothbores from the rifled .22 LR. | Retain the RC92 connection, add the overlooked 6 mm variant, and identify it as Flobert rather than .22 LR. |
| Winchester 61 | **Classification needs precision.** Stepped and counterbored shot-only variants still belong to the smoothbore family. See [American Rifleman, historical account](https://www.americanrifleman.org/content/the-winchester-model-61-a-look-back/) and [Gun Digest's model entries](https://gunvalues.gundigest.com/winchester-repeating-arms/15652/model-61/). | Add a bore-description label, not a mutually exclusive “not smoothbore” category. |
| Mossberg Targo | **The blanket accessory claim is unsupported.** The [NMCA table](https://mossbergcollectors.org/100-years.pdf) distinguishes 42T from 42TR. Mossberg's [May 1961 factory advertisement, printed p. 6](https://gunsmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/G0561.pdf#page=6), documents the accessories for the 320TR/340TR revival. | Keep the existing model-specific wording; do not claim every earlier Targo came with a rifled adapter. |

## Additional omissions and leads

- **Confirmed addition — Remington Model 33 Smooth Bore.** [Remington's archived history](https://web.archive.org/web/20250720023830/https://www.remarms.com/firearm-history/model-33) explicitly lists the variant and .22 shot cartridges. The family dates are 1932–1935. This upgrades the original audit's “likely.” A dedicated card is included.
- **Confirmed addition — RC92 6 mm Flobert.** Factory evidence above establishes a named European smoothbore that the audit treated only as a vague category. Its chambering is kept distinct from .22 LR.
- **Confirmed additional variant — Remington 510-X Smooth Bore.** The [factory model brochure, cover](https://classicamericangunsmith.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/Remington-Field-Service-Manual-for-Model-510-X-Model-511-X-Model-512-X.pdf#page=1) identifies a smoothbore grade specifically for the 510-X. [Remington historian John Gyde](https://www.remingtonsociety.org/forums/viewtopic.php?p=1391) dates the parent model to 1964–1966 and leaves the SB total unknown. Added within the 510-family card; this does not establish a 511-X or 512-X smoothbore.
- **Documented specimen, factory designation unresolved — Stevens-Maynard Jr.** [North American Auction Company, July 26, 2025, lot 333](https://www.icollector.com/Stevens-Maynard-Jr-Model-15-1-2-22-Shot-Rifle_i57207622) describes an ex–Tombstone Western Heritage Museum artifact marked STEVENS-MAYNARD JR. and 22-SHOT, catalogued as No. 15½. The record does not establish a bore inspection or factory production listing for that exact designation. Keep this separate from the original audit’s vague Stevens/Springfield Model 15 lead.
- **Unresolved — BSA No. 1 garden gun.** The [UK Historic Arms Resource Centre](https://www.rifleman.org.uk/BSA_Model_1.html) describes a smoothbore variant. The linked circa-1932 brochure image inspected here describes the rifled model and does not independently establish the smoothbore offering. This is a research lead, not a new confirmed card.
- **Unresolved — Marlin 80 CSB / 101SB.** The [index to the 1945 Marlin catalog](https://cornellpubs.com/product/1598/) names **80 CSB**, not just 80SB, and 101 DL rather than 101SB. An index is not the original catalog page. The [McCracken Library's 1946 Marlin price list finding aid](https://mccrackenatcenterofthewest.libraryhost.com/repositories/2/archival_objects/75539) is a further lead. Do not collapse similar model names or treat the finding aid as inspected content.
- **Confirmed registry entries, factory origin unresolved.** France's [RGA dataset](https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/referentiel-general-des-armes), November 20, 2026 export, records smooth, rimfire configurations for Anschütz 1370 in .22 Long Shot ([BU900](https://tabular-api.data.gouv.fr/api/resources/96d4d178-02cf-4846-9772-09bf7c319a75/data/?referenceRGA__exact=BU900)), Langenhan Modell 30 in .22 LR ([BW458](https://tabular-api.data.gouv.fr/api/resources/96d4d178-02cf-4846-9772-09bf7c319a75/data/?referenceRGA__exact=BW458)), and Manufrance Ejector in .22 LR ([CG337](https://tabular-api.data.gouv.fr/api/resources/96d4d178-02cf-4846-9772-09bf7c319a75/data/?referenceRGA__exact=CG337)). A separate Ejector entry names 6 mm Flobert ([BK173](https://tabular-api.data.gouv.fr/api/resources/96d4d178-02cf-4846-9772-09bf7c319a75/data/?referenceRGA__exact=BK173)). A registry entry alone does not prove factory production rather than an individual configuration. These remain catalog-research leads.
- **Unresolved — Anschütz 1414.** Owner accounts include a partially rifled example. No inspected factory page settles the variant. Neither the 1414 claim nor an unproved 1416 smoothbore is promoted to the hall.

## Limits and corrections to the method

**Cody letters:** the audit's proposed letter for the circa-1940 Model 62 specimens is not supported by the [Cody Firearms Records Office coverage table](https://centerofthewest.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/SerialNumberRanges2024-Winchester.pdf#page=2). Check the current table before suggesting a paid letter. The inspected Model 62A range is 151251–167688 and is marked serialization records only; the two cited numbers are outside that range. Dating a receiver and proving its original barrel configuration are different tasks.

**Prices and rarity:** the original “Holy Grail / Prize / Solid Find / Bargain Beater” tiers, “cheapest,” and “most findable” rankings were not established by comparable dated completed sales. They are not added to the cards. Existing price snapshots elsewhere in the hall were not comprehensively reappraised by this review.

**Coverage:** no source inspected establishes a complete worldwide model list. Original catalogs for the unresolved overseas and Marlin entries, and variant-specific production records, remain the most useful next evidence. A search without results establishes only that the search was unsuccessful.

**Implementation:** six new photographic cards (33, 341SB, 580SB, 62 Smooth Bore, Mark I-GSB, RC92 6 mm), expanded 572 history, corrected existing cards and finale, synchronized model table and timeline, and the Gallery Hall's Model 62 cross-reference. The collection now has 23 exhibits plus its finale, including clearly labeled non-.22 LR relatives. This is an exhibit count, not a count of proven factory .22 model designations.
